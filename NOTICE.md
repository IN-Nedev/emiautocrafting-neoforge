EMI Autocrafting for Minecraft 1.21.1

Derivative port of digestlotion/emiautocrafting, revision
1a4887ef35d28a66c0e05dad1b1e01e916a08ff8 (GPL-3.0-only).
Original author: digestlotion. Original acknowledgement: All credits go to Emi/EMI.
Upstream: https://github.com/digestlotion/emiautocrafting

The original single-key MaterialNode traversal and EMI fill concept have been
reworked into a client-tick controller with finite targets, guarded input,
inventory verification and NeoForge/Mojang mappings. Original attribution and
GPL-3.0-only licensing are preserved. See LICENSE for the full license.

EMI (MIT, Emily Ploszaj and contributors) is a separately installed dependency.
GTNH NotEnoughItems was reviewed as a behavioural reference; its code is not bundled.
